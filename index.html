Support

Humaura calculates your Human Design chart on your device and turns it into a daily practice: the Decision Compass, the Birth Time Finder, your daily energy, connection charts and a complete library.

Questions

How accurate is my chart? Planet positions come from NASA JPL’s DE440 ephemeris (1850–2149), accurate to about 2 arc-seconds. Birth times are converted with the real time-zone history of your birthplace. See Chart › Deeper › How this was calculated.

I don’t know my birth time. Choose “Don’t know” or “Roughly”. Humaura shows what in your chart is certain, and the Birth Time Finder helps narrow down the rest.

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Humaura Plus. You’ll get a reminder the day before a free trial ends.

How do I restore a purchase? Tap “Restore purchases” on the Humaura Plus screen, or in Learn › Settings (gear) › Restore purchases.
