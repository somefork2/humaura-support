Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

Soulprint is built so that your personal information stays with you.

What we collect

Nothing. Soulprint has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not operate servers that receive your data.

What stays on your device

The names, birth dates, times and places you enter, your decisions, check-ins and settings are stored only on your device (in the app’s private storage, shared with Soulprint’s own widgets). Charts, transits and the Guide’s answers are calculated on the device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Place search

When you search for a birthplace, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find the place and its time zone, under Apple’s privacy policy. Soulprint does not receive or keep it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Sharing

If you share a chart, the link or image contains only what you choose to share (a name, birth date, time and place) and goes wherever you send it.

Children

Soulprint does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
